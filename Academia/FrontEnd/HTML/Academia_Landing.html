Executive Summary: Why Academia?

Schools often rely on separate websites and tools for academic records and communication. Academia is a school management system that puts grades, attendance, announcements, and student information in one place. It gives students, parents, and school staff one system to use, improving communication, organization, and efficiency within the school community.

Target Users: Made For Everyone

Students Parents School Staff
Access your academic world: View live grades, calculate 4.0 GPA, check attendance, and read school news.
Stay engaged and informed: View real-time grades and daily attendance records about your child.
Streamlined management: Enter student grades, record daily attendance, and publish school-wide announcements.

Key Features

  • Secure Login: Role-based access for Students, Parents, and School Staff.
  • Grade Management: Fast course grade tables with instant updates via SQLite3 backend.
  • GPA Calculation: Automatic 4.0 GPA calculation powered by C backend logic.
  • Attendance Tracking: Real-time Present and Absent status logs.
  • Announcements: School-wide news and notice board for everyone.
  • Modular Design: Built with a reliable C core ready for future expansion.

Unique Benefits

  • Improved Communication: Keeps students, parents, and teachers connected in one unified ecosystem.
  • Greater Organization: All school records stored centrally in one relational SQLite database.
  • Easy Accessibility: Lightweight interface that runs smoothly without special hardware.
  • Higher Efficiency & Stronger Security: Lightning-fast C logic with protected role-based portals.

Future Possibilities: Schedules, Messaging, Weather Updates, Advanced Reporting, and Mobile Support.
