Why Your School Needs Academia

Schools often use too many separate websites for grades, attendance, and messages. Academia puts grades, GPA calculations, attendance, and announcements all in one place so students, parents, and teachers can save time and stay connected.

Made For Everyone

  • Students: View course grades, check automatic 4.0 GPA, check attendance, and read school news.
  • Parents: View your child's grades and attendance and read school notices.
  • School Staff: Enter student grades, record daily attendance, and post announcements.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Secure role-based login for Students, Parents, and Staff
  • Automatic GPA calculation and grade management
  • Daily attendance tracking
  • Instant school-wide announcements
  • Improved communication, greater organization, and higher efficiency
