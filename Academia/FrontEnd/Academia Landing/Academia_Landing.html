Executive Summary: Why Academia?

Schools often rely on separate websites and tools for academic records and communication. Academia is a school management system that puts grades, attendance, announcements, and student information in one place. It gives students, parents, and school staff one system to use, improving communication, organization, and efficiency within the school community.

Target Users: Made For Everyone

Students Parents School Staff
View grades, GPA, attendance, and school news in one place. View grades and attendance information about their child. Enter grades, record daily attendance, and post announcements.

Key Features

  • Secure Login: Role-based access for Students, Parents, and Staff.
  • Grade Management: Simple course grade tables and updates.
  • GPA Calculation: Automatic 4.0 GPA calculation powered by C backend logic.
  • Attendance Tracking: Daily Present and Absent status logs.
  • Announcements: School-wide news and notices for everyone.
  • Modular Design: Built with a reliable core ready for future expansion.

Unique Benefits

  • Improved Communication: Keeps students, parents, and teachers connected.
  • Greater Organization: All school records stored in one SQLite database.
  • Easy Accessibility: Simple interface that runs without special hardware.
  • Higher Efficiency & Stronger Security: Fast C logic and role-based portals.

Future Possibilities: Schedules, Messaging, Weather Updates, Database Support, and Graphical Interface.
